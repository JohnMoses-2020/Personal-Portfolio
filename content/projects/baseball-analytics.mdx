## Overview

An exploratory analytics project built around publicly available baseball
data, looking at how player performance interacts with ballpark environments
and how the game's statistics evolve over time.

<Callout type="tip" title="Status">
  Proposed project. No analyses or results are published yet; the sections
  below outline the intended shape of the work.
</Callout>

## Problem statement

Raw baseball statistics are abundant, but context is scarce. The same batting
line means something different in a hitter-friendly park than in a pitcher's
park, and comparing players across eras requires careful normalization.

## Constraints

- Use only publicly available, appropriately licensed data sources.
- Pipelines must be reproducible end-to-end from raw data.
- Visualizations should be readable by fans, not just analysts.
