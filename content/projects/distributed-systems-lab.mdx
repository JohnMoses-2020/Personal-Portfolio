  A personal sandbox for building small, deliberately breakable systems to
  study the behaviours that are hard to observe in production: partitions,
  retries, backpressure, and recovery.
</Lead>

## Overview

The lab is a collection of focused experiments rather than a single product.
Each one isolates a distributed-systems concept, implements it minimally, and
then breaks it on purpose to see what happens.

<Callout type="tip" title="Status">
  This is a proposed project. The experiments listed below are planned, not
  completed, and no repository is published yet.
</Callout>

## Problem statement

Many distributed-systems concepts are easy to describe and surprisingly hard
to internalize. Building and breaking small systems is the most reliable way
to turn textbook knowledge into intuition.

## Constraints

- Each experiment should run locally on a laptop or a small cluster.
- Failure injection must be deterministic enough to reproduce.
- Results must be measurable, not anecdotal.

## Architecture and approach

Planned experiments:

1. **Leader election** — compare lease-based and consensus-based approaches
   under partition.
2. **Retry storms** — model how naive retries amplify load, then add jitter,
   budgets, and circuit breakers.
3. **Backpressure** — observe queue behaviour under sustained overload with
   and without admission control.
4. **Replication & consistency** — trace read-your-writes guarantees across
   replicas with injected lag.
