## Why fleets are different

The per-cluster mental model stops working once there are enough clusters that
no one person can hold their state in their head. Configuration drift,
version skew, and uneven capacity become the default rather than the exception.

## Roll out like you expect to roll back

Every rollout should assume it might be wrong. A pattern that generalizes
well is a small canary slice, an automated health gate, and then progressive
widening.

```yaml title="rollout-stages.yaml"
stages:
  - name: canary
    clusters: 1
    gate: error-rate < 0.1% for 15m
  - name: early
    clusters: 10%
    gate: error-rate < 0.1% for 30m
  - name: broad
    clusters: 100%
```
