## Isolation layers

Namespaces, network policies, resource quotas, and scheduling constraints
each isolate a different thing. None of them isolate everything.

```yaml title="quota.yaml"
apiVersion: v1
kind: ResourceQuota
metadata:
  name: team-a
  namespace: team-a
spec:
  hard:
    requests.cpu: "40"
    requests.memory: 160Gi
    pods: "200"
```
